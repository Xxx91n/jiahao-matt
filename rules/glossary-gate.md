**A term is the user's decision.** Before a term enters `GLOSSARY.md` — a new
term, a rename, or a changed definition — stop and show the user three things,
in the user's own language:
1. the term
2. one sentence saying what it is, the way a person would say it out loud
3. the everyday words it replaces, for `_Avoid_`

Wait for the answer. Write the term only after the user agrees. The user's
answer decides the term, not you.

When the user already said it plainly, keep their words; do not swap in a
fancier synonym. If a definition only makes sense after the reader stops to
decode it, rewrite it in everyday language.
